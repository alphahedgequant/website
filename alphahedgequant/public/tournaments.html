How the board updates

Lichess broadcast listings refresh every 60 seconds; Devpost attempts every 30 minutes. When browser CORS blocks a provider, our same-site read-only bridge is tried; if that also fails, a dated JSON snapshot is used when available, otherwise just the verified/labelled curated index. Statuses follow event dates unless an ongoing chess round is confirmed. Results and complete participant lists live with the organiser.
